Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?

When you first open the app, you'll be prompted to create an account. Simply enter your name, email address, and age range. You can also add profile information such as products you've used and your journey start date.

Is my data private?

Yes! Your privacy is very important to us. Your daily stories (photos and captions) are stored privately and are only accessible to you. We never share your personal information with third parties without your explicit consent. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.

How does the AI Coach work?

Our AI Coach uses OpenAI technology to provide personalized guidance based on your profile information and progress. You can chat with the AI Coach anytime to get advice, motivation, and custom plans tailored to your journey.

What are subscriptions and how do they work?

Face & Embrace offers in-app subscriptions that provide access to premium features. Subscriptions are handled through Apple's payment system, ensuring secure transactions. You can manage your subscription through your Apple ID settings in the App Store.

Can I share my testimonials?

Yes! We welcome testimonials from our users. When you submit a testimonial, it will be reviewed by our team for approval before being published on the app. This helps us maintain quality and authenticity.

How do I track my progress?

Your progress is automatically tracked as you use the app. You can view your milestones, badges, and achievements in your profile. Daily stories help document your journey over time.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account at any time through the App settings. When you delete your account, all your personal information will be deleted or anonymized in accordance with our Privacy Policy, except where we are required to retain it by law.

I'm having technical issues. What should I do?

If you're experiencing technical difficulties, please contact us at pepperlabs8@gmail.com with a description of the issue, your device information, and any error messages you may have encountered. We'll do our best to help resolve the problem quickly.

How can I update my profile information?

You can update your profile information at any time through the App settings. Simply navigate to your profile and edit the fields you'd like to change. Your changes will be saved automatically.
